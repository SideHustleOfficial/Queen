My Queen, My Lord

✦ ✦ ✦

Happy Princess Day. This little corner of the internet is only for you.

My Queen,

Happy Princess Day. I call you my Queen Lord, and I mean it in every way: you rule my thoughts, you carry the weight of my days with a grace I don't deserve, and you still make me feel like the lucky one in every room we share.

Today is yours. You have always been the kind of person who makes ordinary moments feel worth remembering: the way you laugh at small things, the way you look out for the people you love, the way you make me want to be a little better every day. If there were a crown big enough for all of that, I would put it on your head myself.

I hope you feel as cherished today as you make me feel. I hope nothing heavy reaches you, and that everything good finds its way to you. And I hope you know that whatever kingdom we build together, I am honored to stand beside you in it.

I love you, my Queen. Today, tomorrow, and every day after.

Forever yours,
Your Lord
